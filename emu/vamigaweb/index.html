Stronkie's Snake – free demo V1.1

Amiga 500 · levels 1–3
▶ Play in browser
  • Cursor keys steer, Space or Return = fire / start
  • P pauses, Esc in the pause goes back to the title

Stronkie's Pipedream – free demo V1.0

Amiga 500 · world 1, levels 1–10
▶ Play in browser
  • Mouse: left button turns a pipe right, right button turns it left
  • Or cursor keys to move the frame and Space to turn; U undo, H hint
